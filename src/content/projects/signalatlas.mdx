## What I built

I am developing a workflow that collects unstructured customer discussions, extracts pain points, groups related problems, and presents opportunity areas for further research.

## Evidence and outcome

The available evidence is the architecture and current product material. The workflow is functional in development, but it has not been publicly launched or validated as a market-research product.

## Technical approach

The system separates collection, language-model extraction, grouping, and scoring into distinct stages. This makes each stage easier to inspect while the product is being developed.

## Next step

The next step is to test the workflow against real research questions and improve the product before considering a public release.
