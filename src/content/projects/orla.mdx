## Overview

**Orla.co** is a boutique digital fashion platform for modern Indonesian women seeking modest, versatile, and elegant everyday apparel. It combines an editorial storefront with practical local commerce workflows, including BCA bank transfer and QRIS payments, delivery location selection, and multi-courier shipping.

<PublicImage src="/previews/orla.jpg" alt="Orla.co storefront homepage" />

## Problem

The client needed more than a product catalog. They needed a storefront that reflected a luxury brand while supporting the realities of Indonesian commerce: manual payment confirmation, changing delivery rates, and stock that must remain accurate while an order is being completed.

## Solution

- **Editorial storefront** — hero carousel, category filtering, search, and curated collection grids designed around the brand's visual identity.
- **Order-first checkout** — creates the order and reserves inventory before the customer uploads payment proof, preventing overselling during manual payment flows.
- **Local logistics integration** — compares courier rates, accepts a map-based delivery location, and keeps shipment details connected to the order.
- **Clear customer journey** — separates checkout, payment confirmation, account history, and shipment tracking into focused steps.
- **Reliable inventory lifecycle** — locks stock when an order is placed and restores it when an order is cancelled or expires.

### Checkout & Stock Reservation Lifecycle

<PublicImage src="/projects/orla/checkout-lifecycle.png" alt="Orla.co checkout lifecycle from order creation and stock reservation through payment confirmation and delivery" />

The order flow protects inventory at the moment an order is created. Payment confirmation and fulfilment can then happen asynchronously without allowing two customers to claim the same stock.

### Client and Server Boundaries

<PublicImage src="/projects/orla/seam-boundaries.png" alt="Orla.co client and server boundaries separating browser features from server-only data and services" />

The application keeps sensitive operations and server-only dependencies away from the browser. This makes the client experience lighter and reduces the chance of exposing secrets or operational logic.

## Tech Stack

- **Framework**: Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript
- **Data**: Prisma and PostgreSQL
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS, EB Garamond, and Hanken Grotesk
- **Commerce**: persistent shopping bag, product variants, and inventory validation
- **Geospatial and logistics**: Leaflet and Biteship for delivery selection, rates, and tracking
- **Payments**: bank-transfer and QRIS payment confirmation flow
- **Testing**: Vitest covering checkout, inventory, authentication, fulfilment, and integrations

## Key Features

### Customer Experience

- Editorial homepage with hero carousel, category filters, search, and responsive collection grids
- Product pages with galleries, colour variants, sizes, and fabric-care details
- Persistent shopping bag with live stock validation
- Map-based delivery location selection with real-time courier rates
- Dedicated payment confirmation view with deadline, bank details, QRIS display, and receipt upload
- Customer account with order history, status timeline, and shipment tracking

### Business Operations

- Product and variant management for the catalogue
- Order fulfilment workflow for payment review and shipment preparation
- Courier booking and tracking details connected to each order
- Separate access controls for customer-facing and business-facing workflows

## Engineering Decisions

- **Reserve stock at order creation** so offline payment does not create competing claims for the same item.
- **Separate order creation from payment confirmation** so the customer can complete a familiar local payment flow without making inventory state ambiguous.
- **Use one logistics integration** to present consistent delivery options while keeping courier-specific complexity out of the shopping experience.
- **Keep sensitive operations server-side** to protect credentials and reduce browser-side complexity.
