## What I built

I built the storefront, product browsing experience, checkout flow, customer account area, and admin workflows around the client's business needs. The site supports local payment methods and delivery options rather than assuming a generic international checkout.

The key operational requirement was protecting limited inventory while payment proof is reviewed. An order reserves stock first; payment confirmation and fulfillment follow as separate steps.

## My role

I handled the full-stack implementation, including the customer-facing website, backend data flows, checkout and stock reservation behavior, payment-proof workflow, delivery integration, and admin operations.

## Evidence and outcome

The evidence includes three representative product screens and the current implementation. Orla is a functional staging build awaiting client deployment; it is not described as a live production site.

## How it works

Customers create an order before uploading payment proof. The backend reserves available stock during order creation, then staff can review payment and continue fulfillment. A delivery integration provides courier options and shipment updates for Indonesian addresses.

## Technical decisions

- I separated order creation from payment verification so stock state remains clear while a transfer or QRIS receipt is reviewed.
- I used conditional stock updates so competing customers cannot reserve more units than are available.
- I kept payment, database, and courier operations on the server rather than exposing them to browser code.

## Result and next step

The client has a working storefront and operational checkout foundation for the clothing business. The next step is final testing and deployment, followed by catalog and staff onboarding.
