## What I built

The functional build includes the mobile experience, conversational actions, scheduling, goals, habits, memory flows, and voice input. I designed it so people can use natural language instead of filling out several separate productivity forms.

Ningsih turns an unclear thought into a short action while keeping related calendar and personal context available in the same conversation.

## Current status and limitations

Billing integration, broader external-user validation, launch preparation, and the next stage of product rollout are still in progress. The current evidence shows a working development build, not a launched SaaS with validated adoption.

## My role

I am responsible for the product and engineering work across the mobile client, backend data flows, conversational behavior, and third-party integrations. I also define the product decisions that keep the experience focused on reducing the effort required to begin a task.

## Evidence and outcome

The available evidence is a set of real mobile screens, architecture material, and a guided walkthrough. Ningsih is currently being improved through UI/UX work, billing integration with RevenueCat and Google Play, and launch preparation. There is no formal external-user benchmark yet.

## How it works

Under the interface, a language model interprets a request and chooses an action such as scheduling an event, creating a goal, suggesting a smaller task, or saving useful context. A Convex backend stores the resulting state and keeps the mobile screens synchronized. Voice notes are transcribed before the request is handled.

## Technical decisions

- I used a real-time backend so calendar, task, and habit changes can appear across the app without manual refreshes.
- I kept conversational actions separate from the interface so a request can update structured product data instead of returning text only.
- I designed smaller task suggestions and recovery flows around the product goal of helping users regain momentum without punitive streak behavior.

## Result and next step

The current build demonstrates the core product direction and the main interaction loop. The next step is to finish the experience and billing work, then test the product with people beyond my own development workflow.
