## What I built

I trained an ensemble of three transfer-learned image classifiers and combined their predictions with majority voting to recognize 22 Wayang Kulit characters. I also built a Flask interface for serving predictions.

## Evidence and outcome

The public repository contains the implementation and evaluation work. The reported result is 99.80% test accuracy on the corrected split; it should not be read as a guarantee of performance on every real-world image.

## Technical decisions

Before treating the score as evidence, I diagnosed that the public dataset had augmented images crossing the original train/test partition. Correcting the split was more important than preserving the higher but misleading score.

## Result and next step

The project demonstrates both an ensemble classifier and an evaluation-integrity check. The next step is testing more varied images and documenting robustness limits.
