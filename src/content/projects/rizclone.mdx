## What I built

I built an assistant that answers questions about my background and work using a searchable knowledge base. It can also handle direct responses, capture contact details, and log unanswered questions for future improvement.

The interface shows retrieved context beside the answer so a visitor can inspect what informed the response.

## My role

I designed and implemented the retrieval flow, routing behavior, tool calls, persistence, and Gradio interface, then deployed the demo to Hugging Face Spaces.

## Evidence and outcome

The public demo, application screenshot, and architecture diagram are the available evidence. The project is deployed and testable by other people. I have not claimed a percentage improvement because no formal before-and-after retrieval benchmark is recorded.

## How it works

The assistant first routes a request to the relevant path. Knowledge-base questions use a search-before-answer flow: the original and rewritten queries retrieve candidate passages, a reranker selects the strongest context, and the language model produces the response. Contact and unknown-question paths use separate tools.

## Technical decisions

- I route direct and contact requests before retrieval so every request does not pay the cost of a knowledge-base search.
- I use query rewriting and reranking as retrieval quality controls, not as a quantified accuracy claim.
- I expose retrieved context to make the prototype easier to inspect and debug.

## Result and next step

Rizclone provides a working, transparent public demonstration of a knowledge-base assistant. The next step is measuring retrieval quality and groundedness with a repeatable evaluation set.
