## What I built

I built a workflow that collects customer complaints and friction from online communities, groups related problems, proposes startup concepts, and evaluates those concepts against explicit criteria. Each stage is designed to keep the evidence behind an idea visible.

The system can produce a structured pitch brief after the research and evaluation steps, giving the researcher a traceable path from source material to recommendation.

## My role

I designed and implemented the workflow, state transitions, data validation, research integrations, and Gradio interface. I also built the open-source demo and documented the engineering decisions in the repository.

## Evidence and outcome

The public Hugging Face demo and GitHub repository show the working interface and implementation. The project was built for the AMD AI Hackathon and should be understood as an evaluated engineering prototype rather than validated startup advice.

## How it works

The workflow uses several specialized stages: collecting source material, extracting and grouping pain points, generating ideas, applying a binary evaluation rubric, writing a brief, and checking the result. The system stores intermediate state so later changes do not silently reuse outdated outputs.

## Technical decisions

- I used a state-graph workflow so the stages and revision paths remain explicit and inspectable.
- I used binary evaluation criteria instead of subjective 1–10 scores to make each decision easier to explain.
- I kept source citations attached to the research output so a recommendation can be checked against the material that produced it.

## Result and next step

VentureForge demonstrates a repeatable way to move from public customer evidence to a structured research brief. The next step is continued testing of the public workflow, not claiming that the generated ideas are automatically validated businesses.
