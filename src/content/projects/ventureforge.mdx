## Overview

**VentureForge** is an autonomous hierarchical multi-agent pipeline built for the **AMD AI Hackathon (Track 1: AI Agents & Agentic Workflows)**. It transforms the unpredictable, vibe-based process of startup ideation into an evidence-first, deterministic discovery and validation engine.

Rather than relying on ungrounded LLM brainstorming, VentureForge concurrently mines real-world user complaints and friction from **Hacker News**, **Reddit**, **Product Hunt**, **YouTube Comments**, and **Tavily Search**, clusters them into thematic market pain points with verbatim citations, synthesizes targeted startup concepts, rigorously scores them against **8 binary Paul Graham rubrics**, and drafts executive **2-Column Bento Grid pitch briefs** validated through an adversarial reflection loop.

The entire workflow is orchestrated with **LangGraph**, persisted via **SQLite state checkpointing**, and served through an enterprise dark-mode **Gradio Studio UI** powered by accelerated open-weights inference (Qwen3-35B served with vLLM on AMD ROCm / MI300X GPUs).

## The Problem: The Hallucination & Validation Gap

Traditional startup validation and AI ideation tools suffer from three fundamental flaws:

1. **Synthetic Hallucinations & Echo Chambers**: Generic LLM prompts produce plausibly sounding business ideas disconnected from genuine market demand, lacking provenance or real customer quotes.
2. **Arbitrary Floating-Point Scoring**: Conventional AI evaluators rate ideas on subjective 1–10 scales (e.g., *"Market size: 7.8/10"*), producing noisy, non-deterministic scores that cannot be audited or compared across runs.
3. **Stale State & Cascading Errors**: When an evaluator criticizes a downstream pitch brief, naive agent loops fail to invalidate upstream artifacts, resulting in contradictory decks and hallucinated URLs.

## The Solution: Grounded Multi-Agent Pipeline

VentureForge replaces subjective guessing with a closed-loop, adversarial verification pipeline governed by strict state boundaries and immutable checkpoints.

### The 6 Specialized Agents

| Agent | Responsibility | Reflection Target? | Invalidation Scope |
|---|---|:---:|---|
| **👑 Orchestrator** | Central supervisor; manages `VentureForgeState`, routes tasks, enforces evaluation gates, executes invalidation cascade | — | Global State Coordinator |
| **⛏️ Pain Point Miner** | Ingests from 5 sources in parallel within SLA budget (5s); deduplicates & clusters complaints with verbatim quotes | ✅ | Invalidates Ideas, Scored Ideas, Pitches |
| **💡 Idea Generator** | Transforms clustered pain points into actionable startup concepts with 3–5 concrete key features | ✅ | Invalidates Scored Ideas, Pitches |
| **📊 Scorer** | Evaluates ideas via 8 Paul Graham binary criteria + fatal flaw detection; outputs `pursue`, `explore`, or `park` | — | Gating Filter |
| **✍️ Pitch Writer** | Writes structured pitch briefs for `pursue`-verdict ideas (problem/solution fit, key features, business model, moat, discovery milestones) | ✅ | Invalidates Critic Reports |
| **🔍 Critic** | Adversarial auditor enforcing 8 binary groundedness & positioning checks (URL provenance, contained fire, competitive thesis, validation questions) | — | Emits Targeted Feedback |

## Binary Evaluation & Adversarial Verification

VentureForge replaces subjective floats with strict boolean decision trees inspired by **Paul Graham** (co-founder of Y Combinator and author of seminal startup essays like *"Do Things that Don't Scale"* and *"Schlep Blindness"*) and rigorous venture capital due diligence:

### 1. Scorer Agent (8 Paul Graham Criteria & Fatal Flaw Filter)

An idea must achieve a **Pursue** verdict by passing the binary rubrics and avoiding fatal flaws before any pitch brief is drafted (**Pursue-First Filtering**):

- **Feasibility Rubric**:
  - *Do things that don't scale*: Can this be solved manually first?
  - *Schlep advantage*: Does it tackle an unsexy operational hurdle incumbents avoid?
  - *Execution timeline*: Can a 2–3 person engineering team build an MVP within 6 months?
- **Demand Rubric**:
  - *Pain point grounding*: Does it address at least 2 distinct extracted complaint clusters?
  - *Painkiller vs. Vitamin*: Is it an urgent, acute bleeding neck rather than a nice-to-have?
  - *Early adopter vein*: Is there a clear, reachable community with urgent buying power?
- **Novelty & Expansion Rubric**:
  - *Differentiated behavior*: Does it offer a 10x improvement over existing manual workarounds?
  - *Expansion path*: Is there a viable expansion roadmap out of the initial niche?
- **Fatal Flaw Filter**: Instant disqualification for regulatory/legal blockades, unwinnable incumbent distribution moats, or severe technical impossibility.

### 2. Adversarial Critic Agent (8 Groundedness & Strategy Checks)

Every drafted pitch brief is audited by an independent Critic agent before presentation:

1. `all_claims_evidence_backed`: Every market assertion traces directly to verified pain point quotes.
2. `no_hallucinated_source_urls`: URLs cross-referenced against scraped evidence URLs.
3. `tagline_under_12_words`: Concise, punchy executive framing.
4. `target_is_contained_fire`: Specific, highly reachable initial user base (e.g., Facebook at Harvard).
5. `competition_embraced_with_thesis`: Identifies direct competitors and incumbent blindspots.
6. `minimum_evidence_sources`: At least 2 distinct primary source citations.
7. `scorer_verdict_justified`: Explicit alignment between rubric scoring and pitch claims.
8. `validation_plan_complete`: Exactly 5 open-ended customer discovery questions + falsifiable milestone.

## Key Architectural Innovations & ADRs

Key architectural decisions are documented across 13 Architecture Decision Records:

- **Strict Downstream Invalidation Cascade (ADR 0008)**: When the Critic flags a defect and routes a revision back to the Idea Generator or Miner, all downstream artifacts (scored ideas, pitch briefs, critic reports) are atomically invalidated to eliminate stale state contamination.
- **Quarantine Segregation Policy (ADR 0006 & ADR 0010)**: If a pitch brief fails Critic checks after reaching `max_revisions`, it is graduated into an isolated quarantined partition with attached diagnostic cards rather than corrupting certified outputs.
- **Pursue-First Filtering (ADR 0009)**: Pitch briefs are only generated for concepts passing all demand and feasibility hurdles without fatal flaws, preserving LLM compute.
- **Hybrid Bento Grid UI + Deterministic Compiler (ADR 0013)**: Eliminates raw Markdown dumps by presenting structured visual bento cards while generating 100% consistent 9-part investor briefs via a deterministic compiler (`compile_pitch_markdown`).
- **SQLite Checkpoint Persistence (ADR 0007)**: LangGraph state transitions persist automatically to `.cache/ventureforge.db` (`SqliteSaver` with resilient `MemorySaver` fallback), allowing runs to be inspected, paused, and resumed.
- **Dual-Tier LLM Architecture**: Configurable fast LLM tier for mining, idea generation, and pitch drafting, paired with a deep reasoning LLM tier for rubric scoring and adversarial critique.
- **Resilient Multi-Source Mining Subsystem (ADR 0003 & ADR 0005)**: Concurrent async ingestion with 5-second SLA budgets per source and engagement-weighted ranking (HN points, Reddit upvotes, Product Hunt votes, YouTube likes).

## Engineering Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **LangGraph over Hand-Rolled Loops**: The state graph abstraction makes cyclic reflection loops, conditional supervisor routing, and checkpoint rollbacks declarative and inspectable.
- **Binary Rubrics over Numerical Ratings**: Replaced arbitrary 1–10 float scores with deterministic boolean criteria, making evaluations reproducible, auditable, and actionable for upstream revision.
- **Quarantine over Hard Failure**: Rather than failing a run when an idea fails adversarial critique after maximum revisions, VentureForge presents the quarantined brief alongside specific critic diagnostics, delivering valuable negative validation signal.
- **Self-Hosted Open Weights on AMD MI300X**: Enabled high-throughput, low-latency parallel inference across all 6 agent roles without token rate limits or recurring API costs.
